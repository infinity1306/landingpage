Last updated: September 18, 2026

This notice explains how Star Chain Labs handles personal data and business disclosures when you contact us through this website or engage our engineering services. The data controller is Star Chain Labs. You can contact our team at contact@starchainlabs.com.

Data We Collect and Purpose

Our inquiry channels collect your name, corporate email address, project specifications, and timeline requirements so our engineering leads can review feasibility, perform scoping, and formulate proposal architecture. We process this data at your request before entering into an engineering service contract or partnership agreement.1

Providing this information is voluntary, but our engineering desk cannot respond with actionable scoping without project context. We never sell, lease, or monetize client inquiry data.

Security, Infrastructure & Processors

Cloudflare provides secure edge delivery and automated threat deterrence. Cloudflare Turnstile validates human interaction without intrusive tracking. Resend handles transactional delivery, and our encrypted communication relays store resulting project briefs. All partners maintain enterprise-grade SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 data isolation guarantees.

Data Retention

Client inquiries are retained for scoping and relationship records, typically up to 24 months after final correspondence, or longer when active development agreements, nondisclosure covenants (NDAs), or statutory tax requirements apply.

Client & Visitor Rights

Under applicable international data privacy standards, you have the right to request access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal contact information submitted through this website. To exercise these rights, email contact@starchainlabs.com.
